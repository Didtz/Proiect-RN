Learn how to care for your plants

General Plant Care Tips

💧 Watering

  • Water when the top inch of soil feels dry
  • Ensure proper drainage to prevent root rot
  • Water less frequently in winter
  • Use room-temperature water

☀️ Light

  • Most plants need 6-8 hours of light daily
  • Position near windows when possible
  • Rotate plants regularly for even growth
  • Some plants prefer indirect light

🌡️ Temperature & Humidity

  • Most indoor plants prefer 65-75°F (18-24°C)
  • Avoid sudden temperature changes
  • Maintain humidity levels appropriate to plant type
  • Keep away from heat sources and cold drafts

🌱 Soil & Fertilizer

  • Use well-draining potting soil
  • Fertilize during growing season (spring/summer)
  • Follow package instructions for fertilizer amounts
  • Repot plants every 1-2 years

🔍 Common Problems

  • Yellow leaves: Often due to overwatering
  • Brown tips: May indicate low humidity
  • Pale leaves: Possible sign of insufficient light
  • Drooping: Check watering schedule and drainage

✂️ Pruning & Maintenance

  • Remove dead or yellowing leaves regularly
  • Prune in spring to encourage bushier growth
  • Clean leaves with a damp cloth
  • Monitor for pests and diseases
